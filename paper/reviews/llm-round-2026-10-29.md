## Points raised by more than one reviewer

- **§6.2 "time follows the size of the removed set, not the size of the store" is stronger than
  the evidence** (ChatGPT, Mistral, Claude): one run per size, about 1.6x run-to-run drift
  reported, two large data points.
- **§6.5 "the provenance cost is the same under both generators"**: the table's maxima are 26 vs
  36 (Mistral, Claude). §4.4 also cites observed maxima.
- **§6.3 query latency**: the reified join was faster (0.56 vs 0.66 ms median), and named graphs
  were counted but not queried, so this is a storage comparison (ChatGPT, Claude, Mistral).
- **Retraction semantics**: one justification per triple means over-deletion when duplicate
  writers merge citation sets, and there is no statement-level retraction (ChatGPT, Mistral,
  Claude W8). The paper already says this in §7.2.
- **No external baseline** for retraction or provenance (ChatGPT's novelty table, Mistral W1,
  Claude W2: compare with a SPARQL `inferredFrom+` property-path closure).
- **Evaluation breadth**: one laptop, single runs, no repeated trials (ChatGPT, Mistral W5, Claude W4).

## Points raised by one reviewer

- Claude W3: "enforced at write time" is a guard in the inference pipeline, not in the store:
  a client can POST a triple with a `propositionGenerated` flag to `/triples`. Scope the claim.
- Claude W6: the §6.1 synthetic correctness test writes a smaller provenance schema than
  production (§3.2).
- Claude W7: §4.1 folds Wikidata qualifiers and references into one shape.
- Claude W1: the store itself (layout, indexes) is barely described. W5: the model case study
  takes about 5 pages.
- Mistral: §3.1 "for that second invariant any single layer suffices" is asserted, not tested.
- Mistral: §5.3 "catalog share fell from 40 % (v6) to 0 % (v10)" hides the rise to 64 % at v7.
- Claude: §4.3 says "Three model sizes" but the table shows two configurations.
- Claude: §6.2 says "4,354 required removals" but also "a maximum of 4,451 triples" removed by a
  single preview; check the units.
- Mistral: emit threshold 0.4 (§4.4) vs ≥ 0.25 in the Appendix B.2 probe, with no explanation.
- Minor (Claude): "object-key" is undefined (§4.4 step 1); confidence is defined twice; explain
  the 2,139 rows dropped between pulling 155,324 and importing 153,185 (§6.2); stray "Preprint."
  and one-word sentence "Standard." (§4.3); add TripleProv (Wylot et al.) to related work;
  `propositionInferredFrom` is named as if it records inference, which the paper itself says it
  does not; the title says the provenance is retracted, but it is the triples that are.
